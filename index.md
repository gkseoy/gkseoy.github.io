## About

I’m a Computer Engineering undergraduate at Dongguk University and an undergraduate research intern at NOSLab. My interests include operating systems, storage systems, and memory management for LLM-based agents. I’m especially interested in how agents retrieve earlier conversations and how decisions about storing and retrieving that history affect response quality and latency.
